Rekall is a study app run by one person, not a company. These terms are short because the service is small.

Using Rekall

You need a Google account to sign in. Keep your material to things you have the right to upload, and do not use Rekall to store or process other people's personal information.

AI-generated content is not checked by a human

Generated cards, the text read out of your photos and PDFs, answer grading, and everything the tutor says are produced by AI models. They will sometimes be wrong. Rekall drafts cards from your notes and then checks them against those notes, which catches a lot, but not everything. Cards made from a topic you describe come from what the model knows: unless the deck you chose has notes filed under it, they are checked only for being standard material at the level you gave, so check them as you study, and report any that look wrong. Do not rely on Rekall as a source of truth for anything that matters — an exam, a diagnosis, a decision. Your notes and your textbook are the authority; Rekall is a way to practise them.

Grading is a model's judgement of your answer. It can mark a right answer wrong. Nothing about your grades is a real assessment of what you know.

Paid plans

A plan turns on AI grading, generated cards, and the tutor. There are two: a monthly plan, which bills monthly and which you can cancel at any time by emailing the address below, keeping access until the end of the period you have paid for; and a one-off lifetime plan, which is paid once and does not expire. A one-off purchase is not refundable and is not tied to a period.

Reading your notes and making cards from them are counted in pages, against a daily allowance: 30 pages a day with a plan, renewed each day and not carried over. Cards made from a topic count as at least one page. Packs of extra pages can be bought on top, are used once the day's allowance runs out, and do not expire. When there are no pages left, notes you add are still saved, just not read. AI grading stops at 500 answers a day, a limit no one studying reaches, which is there to stop automated use; grading yourself has no limit.

Spoken tutoring is bought separately, by the hour, on top of a plan — the hours pay for the speech, and the plan is what turns the tutor on. Hours do not expire: you buy a number of them and they are spent as you use them. There is no daily limit.

Free accounts exist at the discretion of whoever runs Rekall. Without a plan you can still write, import, review and self-grade cards; AI grading, generated cards and the tutor are what a plan turns on.

Availability

Rekall runs on a small amount of hardware and depends on other companies for its AI features. It will go down sometimes. There is no uptime guarantee, and no compensation for downtime beyond cancelling if it stops being worth paying for.

Your data

Your notes and decks are yours. You can delete them whenever you want, and deleting your account removes them. See the Privacy Policy for what is stored and who it is sent to.

Keep your own copies of anything you cannot afford to lose. Rekall is backed up on a best-effort basis by one person, not by a team with a disaster-recovery plan.
