Privacy Policy

Rekall · last updated 26 August 2026

Rekall is a small study app run by one person. This policy says exactly what it stores, why, and who else sees it. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

What Rekall stores

  • Your Google account details — your Google account ID, email address, name and profile picture URL. Used to sign you in and to keep your data separate from everyone else's. Rekall never sees or stores your Google password.
  • What you upload — photos and PDFs of your notes, kept as files, along with the text an AI model read out of them.
  • Your study material — decks, cards, and categories you create or generate.
  • Your review history — for each answer: what you typed or said, whether you typed or spoke it, the grade, and when. This is what makes the scheduling work; without it Rekall cannot tell you what to study.
  • Tutor conversations — the messages you and the tutor exchange, and short notes the tutor keeps about what you find difficult, so it does not start from nothing each time.
  • Your settings — theme, accent colour, study limits, voice preferences.
  • Problem reports — if a generated card looks wrong, the report includes the card and the model output that produced it.

What Rekall does not do

No advertising. No analytics or tracking pixels. No selling or sharing your data with anyone for marketing. No profiling beyond what is needed to schedule your cards and run the tutor.

Who else sees your data

Rekall cannot generate cards, grade answers or speak without sending data to other companies. This is the part most policies bury, so plainly:

  • Photos and PDFs you upload are sent to an AI provider to be read and turned into cards. Do not upload anything you would not want a third party to process — including other people's private information.
  • Your typed or spoken answers are sent to an AI provider to be graded.
  • In voice mode, your speech is sent to a speech-to-text provider, and the tutor's replies are sent to a text-to-speech provider to be spoken.

These providers process the data to return a result. Rekall does not authorise them to use it for anything else. Providers may change; this page will be updated when they do.

Where it lives

On hardware operated by the person who runs Rekall, not on a large cloud platform. Files you upload are stored on disk; everything else is in a database on the same machine.

How long it is kept

Until you delete it or ask for your account to be removed. Deleting a note, deck or card removes it. Closing your account removes everything associated with it.

What you can ask for

A copy of your data, correction of anything wrong, or complete deletion. Email the address below and you will get a straight answer from a person.

Children

Rekall is not intended for children under 13, and accounts are not knowingly created for them.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what is collected or who receives it, the date at the top changes and anyone with an account is told.
