SN1 vs SN2. SN1 goes through a planar carbocation, so the nucleophile attacks either face and you lose stereochemistry. SN2 is one concerted step with backside attack, so configuration inverts. Tertiary substrates favour SN1; primary favour SN2. Polar protic solvents stabilise the carbocation.
